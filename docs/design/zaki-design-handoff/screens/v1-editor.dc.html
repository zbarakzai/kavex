To check on Windows:

2. Quit Zaki and run:

$env:HERDR_APP_BENCH="$env:TEMP\zaki-frames.jsonl"; & "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\zaki\zaki.exe"

3. Collapse a folder a few times, quit, and send me zaki-frames.jsonl from your temp folder. It shows whether drawing is the slow part.
